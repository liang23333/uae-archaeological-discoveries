## 1. Official Government & Heritage Authority Releases

### Department of Culture and Tourism – Abu Dhabi (DCT Abu Dhabi)

* **Sas Al Nakhl Island (Umm an-Nar) 2023–2024 Season:**
  - **Title:** *DCT Abu Dhabi 2023-24 Excavation Season Gains Momentum with Recent Finds at Sas Al Nakhl Island*
  - **Date:** 22 January 2024
  - **Source:** [DCT Abu Dhabi Media Centre](https://dct.gov.ae/en/media.centre/press.releases/dct.abu.dhabi.2023.24.excavation.season.gains.momentum.with.recent.finds.at.sas.al.nakhl.island.aspx)
  - **Key Highlights:** Excavation of over 30,000 faunal bones revealing the Bronze Age diet (fish, seabirds, dugong feasting), circular communal hearths, bitumen with impressions of wood and twisted ropes evidencing Bronze Age boat hulls, and imported Indus Valley and Mesopotamian trade vessels.

* **Al Ain Rescue Excavations & Ancient Aflaj (Kuwaitat, Khrais, Qattara, Hili):**
  - **Title:** *Major New Abu Dhabi Archaeological Discoveries Reveal Sites and Artefacts from Iron Age and Pre-Islamic Period*
  - **Date:** 16 June 2023
  - **Source:** [DCT Abu Dhabi Media Centre](https://dct.gov.ae/en/media.centre/news/major.new.abu.dhabi.archaeological.discoveries.reveal.sites.and.artefacts.from.iron.age.and.pre.islamic.period.aspx)
  - **Key Highlights:** Discovery of a Late Pre-Islamic cemetery (300 BCE–300 CE) in downtown Al Ain with intact 70 cm iron sword, amphorae, bronze and glass vessels; plus an Iron Age cemetery with 35 graves and over 50 ancient aflaj irrigation channels.

* **Ghagha Island Neolithic Settlement:**
  - **Title:** *New Abu Dhabi Archaeological Discoveries Reveal 8,500 Year Old Buildings*
  - **Date:** 17 February 2022
  - **Source:** [DCT Abu Dhabi Heritage Assets](https://dct.gov.ae/en/what.we.do/culture/tangible.intangible.heritage/ghagha.island..aspx)
  - **Key Highlights:** GHG0014 and GHG0063 stone-built room clusters dating back to ~6500 BCE, pushing back permanent domestic architecture in the region by 500 years.

* **Marawah Island & The Abu Dhabi Pearl:**
  - **Title:** *Marawah Island Excavations & World's Oldest Natural Pearl*
  - **Source:** [DCT Abu Dhabi Cultural Sites](https://abudhabiculture.ae/en/experience/heritage-sites/marawah-island)
  - **Key Highlights:** Late Neolithic MR11 settlement, stone architecture, and the discovery of the 8,000-year-old natural pearl (dated to 5800–5600 BCE).

### Dubai Culture and Arts Authority (Dubai Culture)

* **Overview of 17 Archaeological Sites Across Dubai:**
  - **Title:** *Archaeological Sites in Dubai uncover treasures of 300,000+ years*
  - **Date:** 26 May 2024
  - **Source:** [Dubai Culture Press Releases](https://dubaiculture.gov.ae/en/news-and-press-releases/Archaeological-Sites-in-Dubai-uncover-treasures) / [Dubai City Guide](https://blog.dubaicityguide.com/site/archaeological-sites-in-dubai-uncover-treasures-of-300000-years/)

### Umm Al Quwain Department of Tourism and Archaeology

* **The Lost City of Tu'am:**
  - **Title:** *Discovery of 6th-Century Ruins Believed to be the Ancient Capital of Tu'am*
  - **Date:** May – June 2024
  - **Institutional Partners:** Umm Al Quwain Department of Tourism and Archaeology, New York University Abu Dhabi (NYUAD), UAE University, Italian Archaeological Mission.
  - **Key Highlights:** Unearthing of dense tenements, public architecture, and domestic spaces on Al Sinniyah Island identified as the pre-Islamic regional capital of Tu'am.

* **Ancient Pearling Town on Al Sinniyah Island:**
  - **Title:** *Excavation of Late 6th-Century Pearling Town*
  - **Date:** March 2023
  - **Key Highlights:** Oldest intact dedicated pearling community in the Persian Gulf, featuring beach-rock dwellings, diving weights, and extensive oyster shell middens.

* **Al Sinniyah Christian Monastery:**
  - **Title:** *Unearthing of a 6th–7th Century Monastery of the Church of the East*
  - **Date:** November 2022
  - **Key Highlights:** Second discovered Christian monastic complex in the Emirates with a church, refectory, altar, baptismal font, and monk cells.

## 2. Peer-Reviewed Scientific Journals & Academic Monographs

1. **Stratigraphy & Microliths at Saruq Al Hadid:**
   - **Authors:** Archaeological Mission to Saruq Al Hadid
   - **Title:** *Archaeological excavations at Sarūq al-Ḥadīd – 2022–2023 season: Stratigraphy and new absolute chronological sequence of Area G*
   - **Journal:** *Arabian Archaeology and Epigraphy* (Wiley), 2024.
   - **DOI/URL:** [https://doi.org/10.1111/aae.12250](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/aae.12250)
   - **Abstract Findings:** Identifies backed microliths dating to the Wadi Suq period and Late Bronze Age (ca. 1750–1300 BCE), resolving key questions regarding the site's transitional occupation.

2. **Neolithic Lithics at Saruq Al Hadid:**
   - **Authors:** Research Group on Arabian Prehistory
   - **Title:** *Neolithic arrowheads and Bronze Age industry at Saruq al Hadid, UAE*
   - **Journal:** *Journal of Archaeological Science: Reports* (Elsevier / ScienceDirect), 2024.
   - **DOI/URL:** [ScienceDirect Link](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2352409X24002098)
   - **Key Finding:** Uncovers bifacial arrowheads and trihedral stone points demonstrating human presence at Saruq Al Hadid during the mid-Holocene Neolithic period.

3. **Remote Sensing & AI at Saruq Al Hadid:**
   - **Authors:** Ben-Romdhane, H., et al.
   - **Title:** *Detecting and Predicting Archaeological Sites Using Remote Sensing and Machine Learning—Application to the Saruq Al-Hadid Site, Dubai, UAE*
   - **Journal:** *Geosciences* (MDPI), 2023, 13(6), 179.
   - **DOI/URL:** [https://doi.org/10.3390/geosciences13060179](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3263/13/6/179)
   - **Coverage in Nature:** *Archeologists' new AI-fueled Northern Star*, [Nature Middle East, 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/d42473-024-00453-0).

4. **Neolithic Stone Architecture on Ghagha Island:**
   - **Authors:** Beech, M. J., Cuttler, R. T., et al.
   - **Title:** *New light on the Neolithic fertile coast: recent excavations on Ghagha Island, Abu Dhabi Emirate, UAE, and the emergence of domestic architecture in ancient Arabia*
   - **Publication:** *Proceedings of the Seminar for Arabian Studies* (Archaeopress), 2023/2024.
   - **Source:** [Archaeopress Publishing](https://archaeopresspublishing.com/ojs/index.php/PSAS/article/view/1563) / [HAL Open Science](https://hal.science/hal-04372815)

5. **Out of Africa & Jebel Faya Paleolithic Sequencing:**
   - **Authors:** Armitage, S. J., Jasim, S. A., Marks, A. E., Parker, A. G., Usik, V. I., & Uerpmann, H. P.
   - **Title:** *The Southern Route "Out of Africa": Evidence for an Early Expansion of Modern Humans into Arabia*
   - **Journal:** *Science*, 331(6016), 453-456.
   - **Follow-up Studies (2024):** Confirming 125,000–210,000 year stratigraphy at FAY-NE1 rock shelter.

6. **Recent Fieldwork at Sas Al Nakhl (Umm an-Nar Island):**
   - **Authors:** Department of Culture and Tourism – Abu Dhabi Field Team
   - **Title:** *Recent archaeological investigations at Umm an-Nar Island (Sas Al Nakhl)*
   - **Source:** [Academia.edu Repository](https://www.academia.edu/157002859/Recent_archaeological_investigations_at_Umm_an_Nar_Island_Sas_Al_Nakhl_)

## 4. Museums, Cultural Centers & Digital Archives

| Institution | Location | Core Focus & Displays |
|---|---|---|
| **Saruq Al Hadid Archaeological Museum** | Shindagha Heritage District, Dubai | Thousands of Iron Age copper/bronze weapons, snake figurines, gold ornaments, and raw metallurgical slag. |
| **Al Ain Museum & Hili Archaeological Park** | Al Ain, Abu Dhabi | Bronze Age monumental circular tombs (Hili Grand Tomb), Hafit pottery, and aflaj agricultural tools. |
| **Mleiha Archaeological Centre** | Mleiha Desert, Sharjah | Pre-Islamic monumental tombs, royal palace artifacts, horse burials, and Aramaic funerary inscriptions. |
| **Sharjah Archaeology Museum** | Halwan, Sharjah | Comprehensive chronological collection from Lower Palaeolithic stone tools to Islamic pottery. |
| **National Museum of Ras Al Khaimah** | Old Town, Ras Al Khaimah | Late Bronze Age weapons from Shimal, Julfar trade ceramics, and Islamic architectural stucco. |
| **Zayed National Museum** *(Upcoming)* | Saadiyat Cultural District, Abu Dhabi | National museum documenting the full 300,000-year history and archaeology of the UAE. |
